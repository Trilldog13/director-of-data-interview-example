> [!NOTE]

Welcome to the structured hiring framework example for the **Director of Data** role. This repository outlines our end-to-end interview pipeline, assessment criteria, and scoring rubrics. 

Our goal is to ensure a transparent, fair, and merit-based evaluation process that minimizes bias and focuses on core competencies required for world-class data and ML leadership.

---

## 🎯 Evaluation & Scoring Model

To keep our hiring bar objective and calibrated across all interviewers, we use a standardized scoring system for each stage. Final hiring decisions are mapped across four clear bands:

* **85–100:** **Strong Hire** – Clear, structured, and commercially sharp leadership.
* **70–84:** **Hire** – Solid technical and leadership foundation with minor areas to develop.
* **55–69:** **Borderline** – Lacks sharpness or depth in critical competency areas.
* **<55:** **No Hire** – Performance does not meet the baseline expectations for this level.

## 🔄 The Interview Loop

### Stage 1: Hiring Manager Screening (30 Minutes)
* **Format:** Digital Interview via Google Meet.
* **Interviewers:** James Humphreys.
* **Intent:** Establish an initial mutual fit, walk through critical career decisions, and evaluate communication clarity regarding past high-impact data initiatives.

#### 📈 Core Evaluation Criteria (Total: 100 Points)
* **Alignment to the Job (25 pts):** Motivation for joining the technology division and clarity around career progression.
* **Communication (25 pts):** Ability to articulate complex experiences concisely.
* **Experience & Competence (25 pts):** Depth of experience managing technical data teams.
* **Fit for Ocado Technology (25 pts):** Alignment with organizational ways of working and collaborative leadership.

#### 💬 Session Timeline & Focus Areas
* **Introductions (5 mins):** Quick introductions from all attendees and a brief overview of the role and team structure.
* **General Skills & Fit (10 mins):** Walkthrough of CV, key career decisions, major achievements, impactful projects, and handled challenges.
* **Experience Specific Questions (15 mins):** Focusing on ML deployment at scale, driving experimentation (research vs. getting things into production), taking calculated risks, and stakeholder influence.

---

### Stage 2: Strategy & Case Presentation (60 Minutes)
* **Format:** Technical Presentation & Deep Dive (20-minute presentation, maximum of 12 slides, followed by a 40-minute Q&A).
* **Interviewers:** Sam Lloyd, Evin Harley-Murray, Julia Łucka.
* **Intent:** Evaluate strategic vision, stakeholder management, and the ability to drive alignment across cross-functional engineering and business domains.

#### 📈 Core Evaluation Criteria (Total: 100 Points | 20 Points Each)
1. **Opportunity Identification & Strategic Framing:** How opportunities are identified and prioritized to solve complex systemic challenges.
2. **Stakeholder Alignment & Leadership:** Driving buy-in and alignment across diverse, cross-functional organizational layers.
3. **Technical Leadership & Decision-Making:** Architectural oversight, key technical decisions, and managing trade-offs.
4. **Delivery, Execution & Operationalization:** Ensuring successful delivery, adoption, production deployment, and robust MLOps practices.
5. **Impact, Measurement & Sustainability:** Defining, tracking, and iterating on ongoing business value and continuous system monitoring.

#### 📋 Candidate Brief Provided
> "As part of the next stage of your interview process, we'd like you to prepare a short presentation. Please walk us through a specific machine learning or data science initiative that you have led. We're particularly interested in understanding how you identified and prioritized the opportunity, drove alignment and secured buy-in across stakeholders, oversaw the technical approach (including key decisions and trade-offs), and ensured successful delivery, adoption, and ongoing value (e.g. monitoring, iteration, MLOps)."
